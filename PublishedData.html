Published Datasets

In alignment with open science practices, de-identified datasets from the Social Learning and Perception (SLaP) lab are made available here for replication, meta-analysis, and secondary research purposes. If you use these datasets, please cite the corresponding publication.

Life History Strategies and Conformity Dataset (2026)

Associated Project: Fast Life Strategists Conform Less to Normative Social Influence

This dataset was collected to replicate and extend the work of Stamos et al. (2021). The dataset includes measurements of participants' adult life history strategies, demographics, and ratings of two images for which they were told others' ratings.

Life History Strategies and Intrusive Thinking (2026)

Associated Project: Slow Life Strategists Experience Greater Intrusive Thought Frequency, Particularly When They Endorse More Moral Foundations

These data sets were collected to test the hypothesis that due in part to their socially mutualistic nature, those adopting slow life strategies would experience more intrusive thoughts, particularly when they endorse a wider array of moral foundations. This dataset includes deidentified data of participants' endorsements of the original five moral foundations, their scores on the Mini-K, their instrusive thought frequencies, and their guilt and shame proneness, along with their proclivity to take the perspectives of others, and the empathize with others.
